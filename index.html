Nuvabrix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Nuvabrix for Android and iOS (app ID com.nuvabrix.puzzle), published by Nuvabrix Games, handles information.

Information we do not collect

We do not operate any servers for Nuvabrix and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or any account. We do not receive or store any personal information about you.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, hints), your settings (sound, music, vibration) and whether you have seen the tutorial are saved only on your device using the device's local app storage. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Nuvabrix shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the identifier for vendors (the IDFA is used only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • Information about ad interactions and app usage (for example, ads viewed or tapped);
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the SDK.

This information is collected directly by Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy Choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings; on iPhone, use Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Ratings

After you have played for a while, the app may show the standard Google Play (or Apple App Store) rating window. Ratings and reviews are handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; the app does not receive your rating or any personal information from it.

Rewarded ads

Watching an optional rewarded video grants an in-game hint. Watching is never required to play.

Children

Nuvabrix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
