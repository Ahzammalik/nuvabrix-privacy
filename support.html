Nuvabrix Support

Nuvabrix: 3D Cube Puzzle is a relaxing twisting-cube game with 300 levels, from 2x2 to 5x5, for Android and iPhone.

How to play

  • Drag on the empty space around the cube to rotate it.
  • Swipe across a coloured square to twist its row or column.
  • Make every face a single colour within the target moves for 3 stars.
  • Stuck? Use Undo, Restart or a Hint (the next correct layer turns for you).

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Can I turn off music or sounds? Yes: Settings › Sound Effects / Music / Vibration.

How do I change ad privacy choices? Settings › Privacy Choices (where required), or your phone's privacy settings.
